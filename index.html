    How the landed cost is worked out

    Taobao, Tmall, JD, Vipshop via an agent. These platforms don't ship to the Netherlands themselves, so an agent buys for you, receives the pair at its warehouse in China and ships it on. Cost = item + about ¥8 domestic delivery to the agent, plus the agent's service fee and its currency mark-up, plus international freight.

    Pinduoduo. Superbuy doesn't take Pinduoduo links; agents that do (Supplyia, HooBuy and others) charge roughly the low-fee rate, so Pinduoduo is always worked out with the low-fee agent.

    Import charges. Since 1 July 2026 every non-EU parcel under €150 pays a flat €3 EU customs duty per item type, and from November 2026 a €2 handling fee per item type is added. VAT is 21% on item + freight + duty. The figures assume a "tax-included" freight line that charges these upfront; if the carrier clears the parcel instead, PostNL or DHL can add its own clearance fee on top.

    AliExpress. Official stores usually ship free to NL. The AliExpress prices here are listing prices seen ex VAT, so 21% VAT and the €5 duty and handling are added.

    Two or more pairs. Freight is charged per kilo, so two pairs in one parcel cost well under twice the freight of one. A parcel over €150 pays the normal 16.9% duty for shoes instead of €3, though.

    How it works

    A scout checks sale pages of Dutch and European running shops every morning (Zalando, Decathlon, Boozt, Top4Running, All4running, Breuninger, SportsShoes, i-run, 21RUN) and the brands' own stores (Nike, adidas, HOKA, Puma, Mizuno, Brooks, Salomon) and refreshes this list. Deals stay up until the next scan, so a price or size can change in between: the shop's page has the final word.

    Size status. Green means the scout saw your size in stock. Amber means the shop's sale page didn't show stock per size, so pick your size at the shop. Deals confirmed out of your size are hidden.

    "Was" prices. RRP is the brand's advised price. Under the EU Omnibus rule many shops show a 30-day average or the lowest price of the last 30 days instead; each card says which one the discount is based on.

    Affiliate links. Some links (currently Salomon and Direct Running) are affiliate links: if you buy through them, Negative Split may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. It never changes which deals are shown or how they are ranked.
