    How it works

    A scout checks sale pages of Dutch and European running shops every morning (Zalando, Decathlon, Boozt, Top4Running, All4running, Breuninger, SportsShoes, i-run, 21RUN) and the brands' own stores (Nike, adidas, HOKA, Puma, Mizuno, Brooks, Salomon) and refreshes this list. Deals stay up until the next scan, so a price or size can change in between: the shop's page has the final word.

    Size status. Green means the scout saw your size in stock. Amber means the shop's sale page didn't show stock per size, so pick your size at the shop. Deals confirmed out of your size are hidden.

    "Was" prices. RRP is the brand's advised price. Under the EU Omnibus rule many shops show a 30-day average or the lowest price of the last 30 days instead; each card says which one the discount is based on.
